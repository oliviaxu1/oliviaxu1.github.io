## Research Overview

This study examines whether China's Digital RMB (e-CNY) can address the privacy and market concentration issues associated with third-party payment platforms. As digital payment services have become increasingly dominant in China, concerns have emerged regarding user privacy, data security, and the high concentration of market power. This research evaluates how the institutional design of the Digital RMB may contribute to a more secure, transparent, and competitive payment ecosystem.

The study adopts a qualitative research approach combining literature review, policy analysis, and comparative analysis to evaluate the Digital RMB against existing third-party payment platforms and discuss its future development and policy implications.

## Research Motivation

China's mobile payment market is highly concentrated, with third-party payment platforms dominating everyday transactions. Although these platforms have significantly improved payment efficiency, they have also raised concerns regarding user privacy, data security, and market competition. The introduction of the Digital RMB provides an opportunity to explore whether a central bank digital currency can offer a safer, more transparent, and more competitive payment system.

## Key Findings

- The Digital RMB has the potential to strengthen privacy protection through its central bank-backed institutional design.

- Compared with third-party payment platforms, the Digital RMB may help reduce market concentration and promote fairer competition.

- Broader adoption of the Digital RMB will require continued policy support, technological development, and public acceptance.

- The study proposes policy recommendations to support the sustainable development of the Digital RMB ecosystem.
